# Chorus demo video outline

## Purpose

Show the working collaborative-project prototype without implying that a
reported contribution is money received, that an approval is a payment, or
that a member-reported outcome is independently verified.

## Before recording

- Start from the documented local setup and sign in with a dedicated demo
  account. Do not expose personal email addresses, `.env.local`, credentials,
  browser password managers, or unrelated browser tabs.
- Use a project with an objective, target budget, one manually managed task,
  and a clearly unverified contribution report. These can be entered through
  the UI during the recording; do not describe them as payment fixtures.
- Keep the default AI configuration disabled, or use a configured model only
  after reviewing its privacy tier and account usage charges. AI planning is
  optional and must be described as a draft.
- Do not create or display a real payment, wallet signature, provider offer,
  verified settlement, or live balance.

## Suggested 90-second recording

| Time | Screen/action | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:10 | Chorus landing page and sign in | “Chorus brings a shared project, its people, budget controls, and work tracking into one workspace.” |
| 0:10–0:25 | Create/open a campaign project; show objective and target budget | “A project starts with an objective, expected outcome, and target. Members and roles are scoped to that project.” |
| 0:25–0:38 | Show member/invitation controls and project task board | “Owners can invite collaborators. Members track work and report outcomes; these reports are not independently verified deliverables.” |
| 0:38–0:50 | Enter a contribution report; show reported and verified totals | “A contribution report is explicitly unverified. It does not transfer funds and does not increase the verified balance.” |
| 0:50–1:05 | Show policy controls and an approval record or empty approval queue | “Spending policy and approvals are separate safeguards. An approval reserves budget but cannot execute a purchase.” |
| 1:05–1:17 | Show AI Planning section with the default disabled state, or generate and review a plan if configured | “Optional AI planning can suggest draft tasks. Estimates are not quotes, offers, or spending authorization.” |
| 1:17–1:30 | Show budget position, activity ledger, and integration status | “Available and reserved amounts are shown separately. Verified spending and settlement evidence remain unavailable until a real payment route and independent verifier are implemented.” |

## Closing disclosure

“This is a no-payment prototype. NEAR Intents settlement, MPP service purchase,
wallet signing, provider discovery, and independent payment verification are
not enabled. No real payment or verified expenditure is demonstrated.”
