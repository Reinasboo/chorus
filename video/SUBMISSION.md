# Chorus — Many people. One purpose.

Chorus brings the objective, people, contribution records, budget controls, tasks,
and project activity into a shared workspace. This two-minute film follows a
community art campaign from an idea through collaboration and accountable work.

Repository: https://github.com/Reinasboo/chorus

## Demo notes

Visuals are animated reconstructions of implemented interface features with
illustrative data. The film does not perform a transaction. NEAR Intents funding
is mainnet-only and has not been tested end to end with a real transfer. Its
quote-bound provider status is not independent on-chain reconciliation.
Approvals do not execute purchases; agent service purchasing is not implemented.
Optional NEAR AI planning requires a configured provider.

Voice: ElevenLabs / Chris. Music: original procedural composition.
